Frequently Asked Questions

Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?

No. The form submits a service request. Any appointment must be separately confirmed after provider availability is determined.

Who performs the electrical work?

Electrical work is performed by independent service providers. ElectricianConnect is intended to facilitate service requests and connections between customers and providers.

How much will electrical service cost?

Pricing depends on the provider, location, scope of work, materials and other project-specific factors. Any pricing should be discussed directly with the service provider.

Is service available in every ZIP code?

Requests may be submitted from across the United States, but provider availability and individual service areas vary.

What happens after I submit my request?

Your information can be used to process the inquiry and determine whether an appropriate participating provider is available for the requested location and service.
